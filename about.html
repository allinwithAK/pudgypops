About

A small side quest

PUDGY POPS started with one very melty penguin. We love the Pudgy community, and Lil Pudgy #19587 (two popsicles, one beanie, zero sunscreen) made us want to build something fun around him, in public.

This site is a friendly hub for fans. We're keeping it small, honest and in-progress.
